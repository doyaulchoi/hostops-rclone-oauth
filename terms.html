Terms of Use

These terms describe the personal-use 1060 Drive Backup workflow documented on this site. It is operated by one person for that operator’s own recovery copies and is not a hosted or multi-user backup service.

Authorization and permitted use

Only authorize the OAuth client for a Google account that you own or are explicitly authorized to administer. The client requests Google’s full Drive scope, which can permit access to all files in that account. The current software is configured to use a dedicated backup area, but the permission is broader than that use. Do not use this app to access another person’s Drive data without their authorization.

Your data and backup copies

You retain ownership of your data. The workflow encrypts backup contents and file names locally before Drive upload; directory names and some metadata remain visible to Google. A local plaintext recovery copy is staged until the remote copy and restore checks pass. Verified Drive runs are retained indefinitely by the current automation, and revoking OAuth access does not delete stored copies. You are responsible for reviewing and deleting copies you no longer want and for keeping any required encryption credentials safe.

Availability and recovery

This personal utility is provided without a service-level commitment or guarantee that every backup or restoration will succeed. The automated checks reduce risk but do not replace maintaining independent recovery copies and periodically validating recovery procedures. Do not delete an original or another backup solely because an upload was attempted; rely on a completed readback and restoration check.

Changes and termination

The operator may change or stop the workflow. You may revoke its OAuth access through your Google Account at any time. Revocation prevents future access but does not remove existing files from Google Drive or other copies; those must be deleted from their storage locations.

Contact

Use the developer contact shown on the OAuth consent screen for questions about these terms.
